⚠ The Break Point
"The HCP makes a clinical decision. The patient never knows unless they attend the next appointment."
What breaks the loop
No HCP → App feedback channel
When the HCP renews the DiGA, adjusts the programme, or escalates care — that decision lives in the PVS. It does not automatically update the patient's app experience. The patient continues the same programme regardless of what the doctor decided.
No standardised report format
Every DiGA manufacturer sends different data to HCPs. Some use KIM messages, some PDF reports, some require HCP to log into a separate portal. There is no cross-DiGA standard. This makes HCP review inconsistent and time-consuming.
Renewal gap = treatment gap
If the HCP doesn't renew before day 90, the patient's programme closes. There is no automated renewal prompt, no grace period, and no patient notification before expiry. A missed renewal = unplanned treatment interruption.
Proposed solutions
✓ Bidirectional data pathway
HCP clinical decision → triggers update in patient app. Renewal → extends programme automatically. Programme adjustment → reflected in next session.
ePA integration KIM webhook PVS API
✓ Standardised HCP report
Single-page clinical summary format across all DiGA manufacturers. Embedded in PVS — no external login required.
✓ Automated renewal prompt
Day 80 alert to HCP: "Patient X's DiGA expires in 10 days. Renew?" One-click from PVS or KIM message.
Design Rationale
Two concentric rings
The inner ring (patient) runs faster — daily. The outer ring (HCP) runs slower — weekly to monthly. The visual size difference reflects the frequency difference. Both loops depend on each other but operate at different speeds.
The break point at the top
Placed at 12 o'clock deliberately — visually the highest point, the most prominent position. The gap is not hidden or minimised. It is the central design argument of this diagram.
Animated flow direction
Clockwise flow follows convention. The dashed animation on the break point arc moves in the opposite direction — creating visual tension exactly where the loop fails.
Bridge connectors
Two diagonal lines connect the inner and outer loops — Feedback → PGHD and Decision → Trigger. These represent the data handoff points between the two systems. Dashed = automated but fragile.
Evidence Base
BJ Fogg — Behaviour Model
Trigger × Motivation × Ability. The patient inner loop maps directly: Trigger (prompt) → Action (ability + motivation) → Feedback (reinforcement) → Reward (motivation increase).
Self-Determination Theory
Autonomy, competence, relatedness. The reward node must address all three — not just streak counters. HCP acknowledgement fulfils the relatedness dimension.
DiGA drop-off data
~40% disengage before day 30. Peak drop-off at weeks 3–4. The inner loop friction — specifically weak feedback quality — is the primary driver. Not motivation failure.
